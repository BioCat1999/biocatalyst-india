submission).

## Identity

- **App name:** Biocatalyst India (30 chars — fits both stores)
- **Subtitle / tagline (App Store, 30 chars):** Pharma catalyst tracker
- **Short description (Play, 80 chars):** Track India pharma & biotech catalysts — approvals, trials, deals.
- **Bundle / package:** iOS `india.biocatalyst.app` · Android `india.biocatalyst.app`

## Description (long — both stores)

> Biocatalyst India tracks the events that move Indian pharmaceutical and
> biotechnology companies: regulatory approvals, clinical-trial readouts,
> licensing deals, earnings, and leadership changes.
>
> • A curated feed of catalyst events, filterable by event type
> • Every event links its sources — no black-box headlines
> • Company, drug, and clinical-trial pages connecting events to entities
> • Market-reaction windows showing exactly what was measured, and by whom
> • Watchlists with alerts for the companies and drugs you follow
> • Secure member access with any valid email address, including Gmail
>
> Built for investors, analysts, and industry watchers who need trustworthy,
> source-linked intelligence on India's pharma and biotech sector. Your data
> stays with the operator: no analytics, no advertising, no third-party
> tracking.
